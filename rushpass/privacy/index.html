Privacy Policy — RushPass

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Publisher: Benchwork Tools (benchworktools@gmail.com)

1. Zero Customer PII Storage

RushPass is architected with strict data minimization. We never collect, store, or sell shopper personal data — including shopper names, email addresses, shipping addresses, phone numbers, or payment card numbers.

2. Data Stored for App Operation

  • Merchant Store Record: Store domain / Wix instance ID, OAuth access token (encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3), RushPass widget settings (fee amount, label, tag), and platform billing subscription ID.
  • Order Accounting Ledger: Non-personal order ID, order number, RushPass fee amount, currency, and platform usage-billing record ID strictly for idempotent usage billing and your merchant ROI dashboard.

3. GDPR, CCPA & Platform Compliance Webhooks

RushPass implements Shopify's mandatory compliance webhooks (customers/data_request, customers/redact, and shop/redact) and Wix's app removal webhooks. Upon uninstall and redaction notice, all merchant configuration, access tokens, and order ledger records are permanently deleted from Cloudflare D1.

4. Infrastructure & Contact

RushPass runs on Cloudflare Workers & Cloudflare D1 with strict HTTPS/HSTS and constant-time HMAC webhook verification. For privacy inquiries or deletion requests, email benchworktools@gmail.com.
