How RushPass Works (30-Second Setup)

  1. Install for $0/month: Add RushPass from the Wix App Market or Shopify App Store.
  2. Pick a preset & price: Choose Priority Processing ($3.95), Gift Wrap ($4.95), or Extra Protective Packaging ($2.95). The checkbox is always unchecked by default with clear copy distinguishing packing priority from carrier transit speed.
  3. Enable the cart block: Toggle on the native Wix Cart Widget (powered by Wix's Additional Fees SPI) or Shopify Online Store 2.0 Cart Block.
  4. Ship Rush orders first: Paid Rush orders are automatically tagged PRIORITY-RUSH so ShipStation, ShipBob, Wix Orders, and Shopify Admin float them to the top of your packing queue.

Transparent Usage Pricing

  • Monthly Base Fee: $0.00 / month.
  • Free Monthly Quota: First 5 triggered Rush orders every month are completely free.
  • Usage Fee: $0.40 USD per triggered paid Rush order after the first 5/month, billed automatically through Wix Billing or Shopify Billing with your customizable monthly safety cap.
  • Merchant Keeps 100% of Fee Revenue: When a shopper pays $3.95, you keep ~$3.55 net pure profit.
