RushPass provides merchant-configured cart add-on software (Priority Processing, Gift Wrap, or Protective Packaging) and automatic order tagging for Wix Stores and Shopify. RushPass is a software utility only — it is not shipping insurance, a carrier, or a payment processor. Merchants are solely responsible for fulfilling prioritized orders accurately and setting transparent customer-facing descriptions.

2. Usage Billing & Cancellation

RushPass charges $0/month base. The first 5 triggered Rush orders per calendar month are free; subsequent triggered paid orders are metered at $0.40 USD each via Wix Billing or Shopify Billing up to your configured monthly cap. You may disable the widget or uninstall RushPass at any time with zero cancellation penalties.
