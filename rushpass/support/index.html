Frequently Asked Questions

Does Priority Processing upgrade USPS/UPS transit speed?
No — Priority Processing moves an order to the front of your in-house packing queue so it leaves your facility first. RushPass's default cart copy clearly states this to shoppers so there is zero confusion.

How do I sort Rush orders in ShipStation or my packing queue?
RushPass automatically tags every paid Rush order with PRIORITY-RUSH (customizable). Create a 1-rule filter or sort view on that tag.

What if a shopper doesn't check the box?
You pay $0.00. You are only billed $0.40 after a shopper opts in, pays your Priority Processing fee (e.g. $3.95), and you exceed 5 free Rush orders in that month.
