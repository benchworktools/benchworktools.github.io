Privacy Policy & Data Security Statement

Product: FieldLens — Custom Field & Context Auditor for Jira · Vendor: Benchwork Tools · Effective Date: November 21, 2026

1. Data Hosting & Zero External Egress

FieldLens runs 100% natively inside Atlassian's managed Forge cloud infrastructure. Zero customer data, metadata, or telemetry is transmitted to external servers, third-party analytics platforms, or external sub-processors.

2. Permissions & Scope Minimization

  • read:jira-work — Read-only access to Jira custom field definitions, screen/context counts, and on-demand JQL approximate issue counts. FieldLens cannot delete, rename, or mutate Jira issues or configuration.
  • storage:app — Atlassian-hosted Forge Key-Value Storage used strictly to store aggregate numeric audit trend snapshots (timestamp, health score, total field count, orphaned/duplicate/bloat counts).

3. Personal Data & Issue Content

FieldLens does not collect, process, or store personal data (names, emails, account IDs, IP addresses) and never stores Jira issue titles, descriptions, comments, or attachments.

4. Data Retention, Deletion & Security Contact

Uninstalling FieldLens from your Jira Cloud site automatically removes all Forge app storage in accordance with Atlassian's Forge data lifecycle policy. Report security questions or vulnerabilities to benchworktools@gmail.com.
